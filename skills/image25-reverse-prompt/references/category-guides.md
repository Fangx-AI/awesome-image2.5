# Category-specific visual anchors

## Portrait and photography

Observe pose, gaze, framing, visible clothing, skin texture, light and environment. Preserve the relative size of head, hands and foreground objects. Do not infer identity, ethnicity, camera model or hidden anatomy.

## Product and food

Describe geometry before finish: cap, seams, label placement, vessel thickness and ingredient structure. Then describe contact shadows, reflections, steam or moisture. Do not invent unseen functional parts or text.

## Illustration and painting

Separate subject from technique. Inspect line weight, shape simplification, hard versus soft edges, color blocks, brush texture, paper or canvas, and spatial layering. Explain the observed style rather than relying on an artist's name. Distinguish transparent watercolor from impasto and flat paper from smooth 3D.

## Characters, toys and 3D

Record silhouette, head-to-body ratio, costume construction, pose, facial features and material. Preserve which side carries an accessory. Identify whether the surface reads as resin, clay, fabric, metal or skin; do not simply describe every shiny object as plastic.

## Posters, typography and brand boards

Describe grid, margins, hierarchy, line breaks, letterform geometry and the relationship between text and image. For a brand board, identify which logo, color and typographic features repeat. Keep exact text only when readable and relevant to the user's request.

## UI and screen photography

Distinguish a flat screenshot from a photograph of a device. For UI, describe navigation, columns, spacing, selection states and content density. For photographed screens, add device edges, perspective and actual reflections without inventing interface details.

## Architecture and landscapes

Track horizon, major lines, entrances, furniture scale and interior/exterior connections. Describe depth with occlusion and atmospheric layers. Do not infer a specific building or location from generic visual features.

## Diagrams and technical illustrations

Preserve nodes, labeled relationships, arrow direction, parts order and legend semantics. Unreadable values remain unspecified. Do not infer scientific correctness from polish; reconstruct the visual organization without inventing results.
